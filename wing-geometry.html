What "the burst sits 1 m inside the wingspan" means

A top-down view (looking straight down on the drone). Left–right = wingspan. The dot on the right is where the warhead goes off.

The engine assumes the whole drone is on one side of the burst — every fragment sprays toward the drone. That holds as long as no part of the wing reaches out past the burst point. The question is only: does the near wingtip poke out beyond the 5 m burst, or not?

If we scale by span (12 m span → wingtip at 6 m) the problem

Burst is 5 m out. The wingtip reaches 6 m — so the last 1 m of wing sticks out past the burst (the red sliver). Fragments only spray inward, so that sliver never gets hit.

If we scale by real length (6.5 m → wingtip at 4.3 m) clean

Now the wingtip reaches only 4.3 m — it stops short of the 5 m burst. The whole drone is inboard of the burst, so the validated one-sided model holds with no change.
