Summary read-out

The formatted text summary the tool prints (illustrative values shown).
What this is. A mockup for approval of the look & flow — the animation and scatter are illustrative motion. The physics engine is now built and validated (runs in Octave + MATLAB), so the read-out figures below match the real tool: closing 226.4 m/s, budget fragment-dominated, and the real by-part split fuselage 23 / wing 0 / tail 6.

Why wing = 0. The UAV is a crude placeholder shape (flat plates — 2.5 m long, 3.0 m span, swappable). Its thin wing sits edge-on and is shadowed by the body, and fragments drift ~1.2 m rearward, so hits land on the body & tail. This is a placeholder-shape artifact, not a modelling error — the hit-detection is independently validated. A real drone STL gives the true wing split.
