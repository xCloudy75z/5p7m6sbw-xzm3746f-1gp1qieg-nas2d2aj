Project roadmap

The whole picture on one page: what the tool is, what's already built, what we're deciding now, the one thing we're waiting on, and what we can keep building meanwhile.

The goal. A MATLAB tool (physics core also runs in Octave) that models a head-on air intercept: a proximity-fuzed warhead bursts a few metres from a UAV, hundreds of fragments fly out and strike a 3-D drone mesh. It reports which parts are hit, where, when, and with how much kinetic energy, plus coverage — and renders a 3-D animation. Reviewed entirely as live web pages.
